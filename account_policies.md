# Retail Banking Policies (Sample)

## Account Opening
- Customers must be 18 years or older to open a standard savings or current account.
- Valid government-issued ID and proof of address are required.
- A minimum opening deposit of MUR 1,000 applies to savings accounts.

## Fees
- Monthly maintenance fee on current accounts: MUR 150, waived if the average balance exceeds MUR 25,000.
- Overdraft fee: 2% of the overdrawn amount per month, capped at MUR 500.
- International transfer fee: MUR 350 plus a 0.5% FX margin on the converted amount.

## Loans
- Personal loans are available up to 10 times the customer's monthly net salary.
- Loan applications require the last 3 months of payslips and bank statements.
- Early repayment is allowed at any time with no penalty after the first 12 months.

## Card Security
- Customers must never share their one-time password (OTP) with anyone, including bank staff.
- A lost or stolen card must be blocked immediately via the mobile app or by calling the customer care line.
- Disputed card transactions must be reported within 60 days of the statement date.

## Digital Banking
- Mobile banking locks after 5 failed login attempts and requires identity verification to unlock.
- Transfers above MUR 100,000 require a second authentication step.
